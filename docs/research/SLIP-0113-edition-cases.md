# Edition and multi-disc cases (SLIP-0113 research)

Gathered 2026-10-21 by one web-research pass; the [KC] claims were re-read
at the source the same day. **S** = a source is linked; **U** = no source
found. Sizes are W × H × D in mm. No source gives an RGB value for any
colour here, so every RGB in `core/case_types.py` is judged by eye.

## Most editions change the insert, not the case

PS1 Platinum (PAL) and Greatest Hits (NTSC-U) mark the edition on the
printed insert; no source shows a different case [ES]. The user's PAL
Ridge Racer Type 4 is a Platinum copy in the standard PAL case. PS2
Greatest Hits (NTSC-U) is red on the label, not the plastic. GameCube
Player's Choice (NTSC-U) is a mark on the insert [NS]. These need no case.

## Plastic colours

Each is the console's standard case size in another plastic.

| Case | Plastic | [KC] text |
|---|---|---|
| PS2, PAL Platinum | silver | "silver for Platinum releases" |
| PS2 | navy | "Navy blue or black"; PAL "Blue in PAL regions from 2001 onward" |
| PS2, PAL specials | red, white, clear | "red for games that require Buzz controllers, white for select SingStar games and Gran Turismo 4 ... and clear for Metal Gear Solid 2" |
| PS3, Americas Greatest Hits | opaque red | "Greatest Hits (PlayStation) games in the Americas come in opaque red" |
| PS3, Europe Platinum | silver | "Platinum Hits games in Europe come in silver" |
| Xbox, NTSC-U Platinum Hits | silver | "Platinum Hits (North America) come in silver" |
| Xbox 360 Platinum Hits | translucent grey | "Platinum Hits games come in translucent grey" |
| Xbox 360 Kinect games | translucent purple | "Kinect games in translucent purple" |
| GameCube, PAL | blue, red, silver (Player's Choice) | "blue (Super Mario Sunshine), red (Mario Kart: Double Dash), or silver (Player's Choice titles)" |
| Wii specials | red, yellow, black | "red (New Super Mario Bros. Wii), yellow (Mario & Sonic at the London 2012 Olympic Games), and black (Pandora's Tower in Europe and Japan)" |

Xbox Classics (PAL) are "green" [KC], against the standard "Lime green";
no source tells the two apart, so no case was added.

## Multi-disc cases

| Case | W × H × D | Plastic | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| PS1 NTSC-U double ("fat") | about 125 × 142 × 24 | standard | S [PS], a protector's inside size |
| PS1 PAL multiway, 2 to 5 discs | deeper than 18 | black | depth U |
| Dreamcast double, US and JP | 24 deep | JP white; black on some | S [DJ] |
| PC CD "chubby", 2 to 6 discs | 24 deep | clear | S [OD] |
| PS one Books, NTSC-J | slim jewel, 5 deep | clear | S [TB], [OD] |

The back card's end strip on a 24 mm case is not sourced.

## Not found

The NTSC-J "The Best" lines for PS1 and PS2; Sega Sports black Dreamcast
game cases (the documented black Sega Sports item is the console bundle);
Saturn multi-disc cases; PS3 Essentials and Nintendo Selects plastic.
